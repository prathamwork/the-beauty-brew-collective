# The Beauty Brew Collective

Create an exceptionally premium, production-quality website for a luxury café and salon concept.

PROJECT NAME:

Blush Unisex Salon

TAGLINE:

“Beauty, Brewed Beautifully.”

Replace all placeholders with editable sample content that can easily be updated later.

CORE CONCEPT

This is not simply a café placed beside a salon. It is one unified luxury lifestyle destination where beauty, relaxation, coffee, conversation and self-care come together.

The complete experience should feel like entering an exclusive beauty lounge combined with a sophisticated speciality café.

The café should be presented as part of the salon ritual:

* Guests can enjoy handcrafted drinks while waiting.

* Clients can order beverages during salon services.

* Friends can meet at the café while someone completes an appointment.

* The business can offer combined salon and café experience packages.

* Visitors can also come only for the café or only for salon services.

The website must feel original and must not copy L’Oréal, Lakmé Salon or any other brand. Draw inspiration only from:

* Luxury editorial storytelling

* Cinematic full-screen visuals

* Strong service categorisation

* Clear appointment booking

* High-fashion beauty campaigns

* Premium hospitality websites

* Sophisticated café branding

DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a visually rich, elegant and highly polished experience.

The visual atmosphere should combine:

* Parisian beauty editorial

* Contemporary speciality café

* Premium fashion magazine

* Boutique hotel hospitality

* Modern luxury salon

Avoid a generic salon template, ordinary restaurant layout, crowded e-commerce appearance or overly feminine pink design.

The final website should feel sophisticated enough to represent a premium international brand.

COLOUR PALETTE

Use a restrained luxury palette:

* Warm ivory: #F4F0E8

* Espresso brown: #2A1F1A

* Soft mocha: #8A6A58

* Charcoal black: #111111

* Muted blush: #C7A39A

* Champagne gold: #B79B6C

Use champagne gold only as a subtle accent. Do not use excessive gold gradients.

TYPOGRAPHY

Use an elegant editorial serif font for major headings, such as Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display.

Use a clean contemporary sans-serif font such as Manrope or Inter for navigation, descriptions, buttons, forms and service details.

Typography should be expressive and oversized where appropriate, with generous spacing and carefully controlled line lengths.

VISUAL RULES

* Use full-bleed editorial photography.

* Use asymmetrical layouts inspired by fashion magazines.

* Add generous whitespace.

* Use overlapping images and subtle layering.

* Mix close-up beauty photography with coffee, interiors and lifestyle imagery.

* Use portrait-oriented image cards.

* Add subtle grain or texture where appropriate.

* Use thin borders instead of heavy shadows.

* Keep card corners mostly square or only slightly rounded.

* Avoid excessive glassmorphism.

* Avoid cartoon icons.

* Avoid unnecessary gradients.

* Avoid generic stock-photo collages.

* Never overcrowd the page.

All images must feel cohesive, editorial, luxurious and professionally art-directed.
